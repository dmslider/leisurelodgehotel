Accessibility

Our commitment to guests of all abilities, on-property and online.

Our Commitment

Leisure Lodge Hotel is committed to providing a welcoming, accessible experience for every guest, whether you're staying with us or visiting this website.

On-Property Accessibility

All public areas of the hotel, including ramps, elevators, and the pool area, are wheelchair accessible. If you require an accessible room or have specific mobility needs, please let us know in the "Special Requests" field when booking, or contact our front desk directly so we can prepare for your stay.

Website Accessibility

We aim to align this website with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, including keyboard navigation, visible focus states, a skip-to-content link, descriptive alt text on images, and respect for your device's reduced-motion preference.

Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on this site or during your stay, we want to hear about it. Reach out via our contact page or email leisurelodgeh@gmail.com and we'll do our best to help.
