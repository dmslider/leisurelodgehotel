Event

Conference Room

Designed for productive gatherings and impressive presentations, our state-of-the-art conference room features floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the space with natural light. From intimate board meetings to corporate retreats, every detail is tailored for seamless success.

  • Flexible seating for up to 40 guests
  • 4K video conferencing & AV suite
  • Dedicated event coordinator & catering
Relaxation

Bar

Our signature bar serves handcrafted cocktails against a backdrop of golden sunsets and gentle sea breezes. Whether you're toasting to a special occasion or unwinding after a long day, our expert mixologists and curated wine collection set the perfect mood.

  • Live acoustic music nightly
  • Seasonal cocktail & tapas menu
  • Ocean-view terrace seating
Culinary

Fine Dining Restaurant

Our award-winning restaurant features locally sourced ingredients prepared by world-class chefs. Enjoy panoramic ocean views while savoring dishes that blend international techniques with local flavors.

  • Farm-to-table cuisine
  • Extensive wine cellar
  • Private dining available
Fitness

Fitness Center

Our state-of-the-art fitness center is open 24/7, equipped with the latest cardio machines, free weights, and functional training equipment. Personal trainers are available by appointment.

  • 24/7 access
  • Personal training sessions
  • Group fitness classes
