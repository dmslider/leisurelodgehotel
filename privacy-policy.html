Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

When you make a reservation request or contact us through this website, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, check-in/check-out dates, and any message or special requests you include. We do not collect payment information through this website — payment is processed at check-in.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your booking request or inquiry, confirm reservations, and communicate with you about your stay. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

This site uses cookies to remember your preferences (such as dark mode) and, if you accept our cookie banner, to gather anonymized traffic analytics via Google Analytics so we can understand how visitors use the site. You can decline non-essential cookies at any time from the cookie banner or your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Booking and contact form submissions are delivered to our team via FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. Submitted data is transmitted securely and used only to notify our staff of your request.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you've submitted to us by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to leisurelodgeh@gmail.com or via our contact page.
